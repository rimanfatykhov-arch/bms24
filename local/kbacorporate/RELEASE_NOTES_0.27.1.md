# KBA Corporate Dashboard 0.27.1

This release turns implementation effectiveness indicators into operational navigation and adds management dynamics.

- Open the matching Kanban selection directly from each KPI card and company comparison count.
- Filter the Kanban by company, curator, workflow status, overdue tasks, reporting period and free text.
- Review a 12-month trend of tasks created, tasks completed and average completion cycle.
- Compare the latest confirmed BMS Index with the previous confirmed measurement.
- Export the filtered company comparison, BMS Index change and monthly dynamics to CSV.
- Preserve access scope: curators see assigned programs; authorised implementation administrators may view all programs.

Build: `2026090278`.

After installation, complete the Moodle database upgrade and purge all caches.

Recommended acceptance path: open “Implementation effectiveness”, click the overdue and review KPI cards, verify the corresponding Kanban filters, return to analytics, review monthly dynamics and download the CSV.
