# KBA Corporate Dashboard 0.27.6

This release introduces one role-aware workspace menu across the competency, corporate and BMS implementation journey.

- Students see My competencies and, when tasks exist, My BMS tasks.
- Verified corporate and department managers additionally see Corporate dashboard.
- Assigned curators additionally see Curator students, Task board and Effectiveness.
- Authorised implementation managers additionally see Clients or Client overview, Audits and BMS tools.
- Personal and company work is positioned before curator and administrator controls.
- Assigned curators receive a permanent Curator students shortcut in the Moodle header.
- The same menu is rendered on personal competencies, corporate dashboard, requests, plans, tasks, audits and BMS tool pages.
- Every destination retains its existing server-side capability, assignment and company-scope checks.

Build: `2026090284`.

After installation, complete the Moodle upgrade and purge all caches. Sign in separately as a student, corporate manager, assigned curator and administrator. Confirm that each account sees only its applicable groups and that every visible link opens successfully.
