# KBA Corporate Dashboard 0.17.1.2 — Business Role Management UI

- Build: `2026090102`
- Public API: `1.15`
- Upgrade source: installed `0.17.1.1` / `2026090101`

This release adds the administrative interface for the business-role domain introduced in v0.17.0 and consumed by **My competencies** in v0.17.1.

The UI is company-scoped and covers role catalog, role competency requirements and additive multi-role employee assignments. Global role definitions are read-only for corporate managers. Moodle remains the source of authoritative current competency levels and evidence.
