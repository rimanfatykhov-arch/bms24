# KBA Corporate Dashboard 0.17.4 — Business Role Template Library

Build `2026090201`; Public API `1.15`.

Global business roles are the centrally managed KBA template library. A company creates an independent role copy containing the template profile and storing `templateid` plus `appliedversion`.

Template updates use non-destructive MERGE semantics:

- requirements with `source=template` can be refreshed;
- new template requirements are added;
- requirements changed to `source=manual` are preserved;
- company-only requirements are preserved;
- missing template requirements are not silently deleted;
- employee role assignments are not modified.

The company role remains the input to target-profile resolution and automated audits.
