# Troubleshooting v0.34.0

## No tools are available

The company needs at least two confirmed audits of the same BMS tool.

## No previous audit

The selected audit is the oldest confirmed audit of that tool. Select a later audit or another tool.

## Economic section is empty

Add actual economic values and link them directly to both audits. The user also needs `vieweconomicindicators`.

## Values look unchanged despite different raw scores

The audits may use different scoring scales. The change is calculated after normalisation to percent of scale.

## New menu item is not visible

Purge Moodle caches and verify company access/capabilities.
