# Licence Provenance & Learning Reconciliation — 0.40.1

The company-course view now keeps four facts separate:

1. company course catalogue membership;
2. commercial licence packages and purchased capacity;
3. seat allocation to employees;
4. actual Moodle enrolment/progress/completion.

Bitrix-origin licence packages preserve `externaldealid` and `externalrowid` and are displayed as provenance. The new reconciliation model is read-only and never creates, releases or reallocates seats and never changes Moodle enrolments.
