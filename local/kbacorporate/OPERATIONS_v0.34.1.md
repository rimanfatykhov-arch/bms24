# Operations v0.34.1

## Preconditions

- company access;
- `vieweconomicindicators` capability;
- economic indicator definitions and values.

## Routine use

1. Open **Экономика** from the BMS menu.
2. Select the period, category and periodicity.
3. Select up to 8 indicators.
4. Review KPI cards and trends.
5. Choose an audit to view its economic context.
6. Export the current selection when required.

## Data-quality interpretation

- **Подтверждено** means the value has been marked verified in the registry.
- Missing plan/forecast is shown as unavailable rather than zero.
- Different currencies are not combined.
- A green or red state follows the indicator direction, not simply the sign of the delta.

## Performance guidance

Keep the visible selection focused. The dashboard intentionally limits chart count and trend points. For deeper data work, use the registry and CSV export.
