# BMS 0.8.4 — Recurring Problems & Management Patterns

- Added a BMS24-standard `Recurring problems` management screen.
- Aggregates repeated management decisions by canonical indicator/context binding.
- Shows recurrence count, observed period, action coverage and the latest decision.
- Adds descriptive signals for missing linked actions and recurrence after an earlier completed action.
- Explicitly avoids automatic root-cause, causality or decision-quality conclusions.
- Reuses canonical decision history and tasks; no parallel problem/task/knowledge engine.
- Schema-neutral; ACL/services/privacy unchanged.
