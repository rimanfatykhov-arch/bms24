# BMS 0.8.2 — Management Review & Closure

- Adds a management review workspace over canonical indicator exceptions, decisions, Tasks and follow-up facts.
- Closure readiness is derived; no parallel management lifecycle or duplicate task/KPI storage is introduced.
- Review states: active, awaiting follow-up fact, ready to close, new decision required.
- Follow-up outcome is observational and does not claim causality.
- UI follows the current BMS24 workspace standard.
