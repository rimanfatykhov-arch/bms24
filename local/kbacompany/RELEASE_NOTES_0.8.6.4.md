# local_kbacompany 0.8.6.4 — Management Week / Weekly Protocol

Build: 2026092405

- Adds a read-only weekly management protocol for meetings linked to Management Cycle checkpoints.
- Reuses canonical Battle Plans, Indicators, coordination records, Meetings/Decisions, Finance, Resource Gate and Policy domains.
- Shows previous-week plan/fact, indicators/quotas, coordination changes, current-week plans, finance/resources, draft policy proposals and meeting decisions.
- Generates a reviewable protocol/minutes draft but never saves or approves it automatically.
- Does not create a parallel plan, KPI, meeting, decision, finance or policy registry.
- Existing finance and policy permissions remain authoritative.
