# Acceptance — Metrics 2.2 (rc16)

1. Mark a weekly metric on a position and create/open a Battle Plan for an employee occupying that position.
2. Confirm the metric appears automatically in the Battle Plan without creating a copy.
3. Enter a period plan in Metrics and confirm it appears as "План периода" in the Battle Plan.
4. Enter a fact equal/better than target and confirm status "В норме".
5. Enter a fact outside target on a non-critical, non-worsening metric and confirm "Риск".
6. Mark the position binding critical or create a worsening trend, keep the fact outside target and confirm "Критично".
7. Remove/omit fact or both plan and effective norm and confirm "Нет данных".
8. Confirm the metric drill-down history shows Plan / Fact / Variance / Status.
9. Confirm effective norm changes by `effectivefrom/effectiveto` without changing the metric UUID.
10. Confirm no additional metric, fact, plan or Battle Plan metric tables are created.
