# Release notes — 0.6.9.11 build 2026092205

BMS Company Context Foundation introduces canonical Company Philosophy and Market Assessment domains before the People & Competency line.

The release adds BMS CRUD for purpose, mission, long-term vision, decision principles, market scope/capacity/position/dynamics and market evidence. Business Model and Strategy can pin exact published Philosophy and Market revisions.

A first optional BMS Assistant bridge is included. If a compatible `local_kbaai` is installed, Philosophy and Market EditViews can request structured AI proposals. Proposals never write BMS records directly and remain subject to explicit user acceptance and normal domain validation.

The Market Data Tool Gateway is shipped as a provider-neutral read-only contract. No ISWORK/FNS/Rosstat endpoint is invented or enabled without a separately verified adapter/configuration.
